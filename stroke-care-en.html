Stroke Care

Stroke brings significant life changes for patients and their families, affecting mobility, speech, swallowing, cognition, and daily self-care. Hygiene First combines a professional medical team to provide comprehensive, personalized home care and rehabilitation services for stroke patients, helping them reduce sequelae, promote recovery, and regain confidence in life.

Phase 1: Initial Post-Stroke Care Services

Daily Living Assistance

  • Assisting with feeding, bathing, and transfers
  • Ensuring patient's basic daily living needs are met

Vital Signs Monitoring

  • Close monitoring of blood pressure, heart rate, body temperature, etc.
  • Early detection of abnormal changes

Personalized Rehabilitation Plan

  • Develop initial rehabilitation goals based on patient's condition
  • Prevent complications such as pressure ulcers and infections

Swallowing Training

  • Address swallowing difficulties
  • Provide appropriate training and dietary recommendations

Psychological Support

  • Emotional support and comfort
  • Reduce anxiety and fear

Medication Guidance

  • Assist with timely medication administration
  • Explain medication effects and side effects

Acute Phase Care Focus

The period from several days to two weeks after stroke onset is critical for recovery. Hygiene First provides 24-hour professional care services to ensure patients safely get through the acute phase.

Phase 2: Care Services

Customized Rehabilitation Treatment Plan

  • Combining physical therapy, occupational therapy, and speech therapy
  • Strengthen limb function and self-care abilities

Daily Living Training

  • Encourage patients to participate in self-care activities such as dressing, eating, and washing.

Safe Environment Design

  • Assist with home modifications (e.g., installing handrails, non-slip mats)
  • Reduce fall risk

Swallowing & Speech Training

  • Address swallowing difficulties
  • Continuously improve swallowing and communication abilities

Psychological & Social Support

  • Encourage interaction with family and friends
  • Reduce anxiety and fear

Medication Guidance

  • Continuously monitor medication usage
  • Guide proper medication administration methods

Golden Recovery Period Focus

2 weeks to 6 months is the golden period for stroke rehabilitation. Hygiene First provides professional multidisciplinary rehabilitation therapy to help patients rebuild life skills.

Phase 3: Long-term Care Services

Ongoing Rehabilitation Exercise

  • Regular physical and occupational therapy
  • Maintain muscle strength and mobility

Independent Living Skills Enhancement

  • Assist patients in gradually regaining independence in more daily activities

Health Monitoring

  • Regular vital signs checks
  • Prevent chronic complications

Psychological Recovery & Community Participation

  • Encourage participation in community activities
  • Prevent depression and improve quality of life

Caregiver Support

  • Provide care skills training and emotional support
  • Assist family members in maintaining physical and mental health

Medication Guidance

  • Continuously monitor medication usage
  • Guide proper medication administration methods

Comprehensive Care Commitment

Regardless of which stage the rehabilitation journey is at, Hygiene First provides comprehensive, personalized care and treatment services, allowing patients and families to face the stroke rehabilitation journey with peace of mind.
