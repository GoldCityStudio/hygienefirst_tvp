Private Nursing Services | 24-Hour Professional Personalized Care

Private nursing services are designed for elderly and patients who require personalized care, especially suitable for those who need continuous care and companionship during hospitalization, recovery, or home life. Hygiene First provides services through qualified medical and nursing staff, offering caring, reliable, and flexible nursing support based on the health status and actual needs of service users, giving peace of mind to patients and their families.

Service Content

Inpatient Private Nursing Services

Provide 24-hour ward companionship for inpatients, assisting with daily living needs, including:

  • Daily care and companionship
  • Meal assistance
  • Assisting patient mobility and basic observation
  • Communication with medical staff about patient condition

Suitable for patients who need long-term companionship and whose family members cannot stay in the hospital for extended periods.

Home Private Nursing Services

Provide home nursing support for post-illness recovery or elderly, including:

  • Post-illness care and daily living support
  • Daily living assistance
  • Basic health observation
  • Supporting elderly to live safely in familiar home environment

Specialized Nursing Services

Provide more specialized support for the medical and nursing needs of elderly and patients, including:

  • Wound care
  • Specialized nursing needs
  • Recovery period support and care

Services are provided by nursing staff with relevant experience and followed up as needed.

Personalized Care Service Plan

We will develop a personalized care plan based on each service user's:

  • Health status
  • Medical recommendations
  • Family and living needs

Develop a personalized care plan, flexibly arrange service hours and care content to ensure the nursing services provided meet actual needs.

Suitable For

Patients requiring 24-hour companionship during hospitalization
Post-illness recovery or mobility-impaired individuals
Elderly requiring personal care and companionship
Families unable to provide long-term personal care
