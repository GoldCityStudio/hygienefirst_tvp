About

HYGIENE FIRST

Our Story

Since 2008, the Hygiene First team has been committed to providing quality medical care services for Hong Kong. The Hygiene First team has over 20,000 medical personnel from different professional fields, including nurses, doctors, physical therapists, health care workers, and other medical professionals.

Over the years, the Hygiene First team has successfully matched medical personnel for individual clients, medical institutions, and government departments, participating in various medical care programs, including outreach services and community vaccination projects, establishing a good reputation and credibility.

In addition, Hygiene First is also a licensed medical and chemical waste collector, with an experienced and professional team that can provide safe and reliable waste collection services throughout Hong Kong at any time, helping customers properly handle various unwanted medical and chemical waste.
