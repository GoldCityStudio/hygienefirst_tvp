Home Rehabilitation Care

The Hygiene First team understands that each person in recovery has unique needs. Whether it's post-surgery, injury, stroke, or chronic illness, Hygiene First provides one-on-one, personalized home rehabilitation care services to help you or your loved ones safely and comfortably progress on the path to recovery at home. Professional Team Support: A team of registered physiotherapists, occupational therapists, speech therapists, and dietitians creates personalized rehabilitation plans tailored to rehabilitation goals including mobility, functional training, language and swallowing, and nutrition management.
