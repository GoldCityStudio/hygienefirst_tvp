Service Scope

If the client does not have items for cleaning and dressing the wound,
the nurse can assist with purchasing. The cost of items is borne by the patient.

Clean the wound, remove dirt and bacteria;
Apply prescribed medication; Change wound dressings

Observe whether the wound shows signs of redness, swelling, or increased pain.
Adjust patient positioning to relieve wound pressure and promote blood circulation.
