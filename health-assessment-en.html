Health screening Types

Registered Nurse (RN)

Focus on assessing patients' physical and mental health and medical history, especially suitable for patients receiving medical treatment, with mental health issues, or chronic diseases (such as cancer).

Assessment Scope Includes:

  • ● Physical Condition: such as hearing, vision, mobility
  • ● Psychosocial Health: emotional and social status
  • ● Medication Management: medication compliance
  • ● Nutritional Status: dietary health
  • ● Medical Needs: such as stoma care, peritoneal dialysis, etc.
  • ● Vital Signs: such as blood pressure, pulse, breathing, etc.
Book Assessment

Physical Therapist (PT)

Physical therapists focus on assessing patients' physical condition, mobility, and rehabilitation needs, especially suitable for frail or mobility-impaired elderly, chronic pain patients, and post-surgery recovery patients.

Assessment Scope Includes:

  • ● Medical History & Injuries: past conditions, existing injuries, and surgical history
  • ● Motor Ability: joint range of motion, muscle strength, and mobility
  • ● Balance & Coordination: body balance and coordination ability
  • ● Pain Source: origin of joint and muscle pain
  • ● Daily Function: ability to perform daily tasks
Book Assessment

Occupational Therapist (OT)

Occupational therapists focus on assessing patients' home environment, self-care abilities, and independent living abilities, especially suitable for patients with cognitive impairment, mobility issues, or those recently discharged from hospital. Through comprehensive assessment, we develop personalized rehabilitation plans to improve quality of life and safety.

Assessment Scope:

  • ● Home Environment: safety and potential risks
  • ● Motor Ability: motor control and coordination ability
  • ● Activities of Daily Living: abilities such as dressing, bathing, eating
  • ● Cognitive Function: memory, judgment, and decision-making ability
  • ● Assistive Device Needs: such as walking sticks, walking frames, etc.
Book Assessment

Dietitian

Dietitians focus on assessing patients' health status, nutrition levels, and lifestyle habits, helping prevent or manage chronic diseases (such as cancer, diabetes), and improving health and quality of life.

Assessment Scope:

  • ● Body Composition Analysis: BMI, body fat percentage, muscle mass, and body water
  • ● Blood Test Report Interpretation: analyze relevant blood test results
  • ● Nutrition Level Assessment: comprehensive review of nutrition intake status
  • ● Dietary Habit Assessment: analyze daily eating patterns and habits
  • ● Dietary Counseling & Recommendations: provide personalized nutrition meal plan, nutrition goals, and lifestyle improvement suggestions
Book Assessment

Speech Therapist

Speech therapists focus on assessing patients' speech, comprehension, and swallowing function, especially suitable for patients with speech or swallowing disorders caused by diseases (such as stroke, dementia).

Assessment Scope:

  • ● Communication Ability: language expression and comprehension ability
  • ● Cognitive Function: attention, memory, etc.
  • ● Oral Function: oral muscle function
  • ● Swallowing Ability: swallowing function assessment
  • ● Caregiver Guidance: feeding methods and techniques
