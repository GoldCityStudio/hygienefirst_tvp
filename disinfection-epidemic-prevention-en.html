Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and answers about disinfection and epidemic prevention services

What is HYGIENE FIRST x SAMBO Luminous Self-Cleaning Technology?

HYGIENE FIRST x SAMBO Luminous Self-Cleaning Technology is a patented technology developed in collaboration between HYGIENE FIRST and Sambo. Through nano-doping technology to modify photocatalysts, it achieves efficient sterilization, antiviral functions, and decomposition of harmful substances under visible light conditions, providing up to one year of continuous protection.

Why choose HYGIENE FIRST x SAMBO Luminous Self-Cleaning Technology?

We are Hong Kong's only technology certified by US MRI testing to effectively kill novel coronavirus COVID-19. Hong Kong research and manufacturing, effectively kills 99.9% of coronaviruses and influenza viruses, environmentally friendly and harmless, suitable for use on any material.

How long does HYGIENE FIRST x SAMBO Luminous Self-Cleaning coating last?

HYGIENE FIRST x SAMBO Luminous Self-Cleaning coating provides up to one year of continuous protection, far exceeding the 90-day protection period of traditional photocatalyst. Even during daily use and cleaning, the coating maintains its antiviral effectiveness.

Is the coating safe for humans and the environment?

Yes, our coating uses an environmentally friendly and harmless formula, certified internationally, harmless to human skin and inhalation, complies with EU REACH certification standards, completely safe with no irritating odor.

Which surfaces can this coating be applied to?

Suitable for use on any material, including leather, fabric, synthetic fibers, glass, metal, etc. Applicable to various environments such as homes, offices, factories, restaurants, vehicles, schools, hospitals, shopping malls, etc.

What conditions are needed for the luminous self-cleaning technology to work?

HYGIENE FIRST x SAMBO Luminous Self-Cleaning Technology breaks through traditional limitations and can function effectively under any weak light, including sunlight, indoor lighting, ultraviolet light, etc. No special UV equipment is needed, indoor lighting can activate it.

How is service pricing calculated?

Pricing is calculated based on factors such as venue size, surface area, coating type, etc. General home disinfection services start from $800/unit, commercial venues are quoted according to actual needs. For specific pricing, please contact our customer service team for detailed quotes.

Does the coating need to be reapplied?

HYGIENE FIRST x SAMBO Luminous Self-Cleaning coating provides up to one year of continuous protection. It is recommended to reapply after the validity period expires to ensure optimal protection. We provide regular maintenance services and recoating services.

What's the difference from traditional photocatalyst?

HYGIENE FIRST x SAMBO Luminous Self-Cleaning Technology is Disinfection Coating 2.0 version. Compared to traditional Photocatalyst 1.0: durability improved from 90 days to one year, changed from requiring UV activation to visible light activation, wider application scope, environmentally friendly and harmless with EU REACH certification.
