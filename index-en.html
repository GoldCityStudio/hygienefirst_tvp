Hygiene First Services

Medical and Chemical Waste Collection

Licensed medical and chemical waste collectors provide professional waste disposal services, including clinical waste collection services such as needle boxes, syringes, and other items, as well as chemical waste collection services covering pills, medicines, and hazardous drugs, ensuring safe and compliant waste disposal processes.

24-Hour Private Care Support

Patient care services provide comprehensive medical care, including post-operative care, hospital transfer escort, prenatal and postnatal care, home nursing, physical therapy, occupational therapy, and other professional nursing and treatment services, committed to providing patients with a safe, comfortable recovery environment and personalized care.

Medical Institution Staff Support

Providing professional medical personnel dispatch services for hospitals, residential care homes, and medical institutions, as well as organizing health lectures, community activity services, and outreach vaccination services, committed to promoting community health education and convenient medical support, ensuring efficient services that meet professional standards.

Vaccination

Providing comprehensive vaccination services, including flu vaccines, COVID-19 vaccines, hepatitis vaccines, travel vaccines, and other vaccinations, committed to providing convenient and professional immunization protection for individuals and communities to ensure health and safety.
