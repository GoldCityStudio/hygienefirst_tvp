Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to your questions about HFG Recruitment Platform

Is HFG free to use?

Yes, HFG is completely free for job seekers. Employers can register and post jobs for free, with some advanced features available for premium plans.

How is my personal data protected?

HFG uses industry-leading security standards including end-to-end encryption, Firebase security rules, and regular security audits to ensure your data safety.

How does the intelligent matching system work?

Our AI system analyzes your skills, experience, job preferences, and position requirements to match you with the most relevant opportunities, increasing matching success rates.

What types of healthcare positions are supported?

HFG covers various healthcare positions including nurses, doctors, physical therapists, healthcare workers, medical device sales, and more to meet different professional needs.

How can I contact customer support?

You can contact our professional team through the in-app messaging feature, email at careteam@hygienefirstgroup.com, or phone at 2827 8889.
