Post-Surgery Care

As a child, partner, or family member, when your loved one needs long-term or temporary intensive care after surgery, as a caregiver you may feel helpless and worried about being stretched thin. Watching them struggle on the road to recovery, your heart tightens, longing to provide them with the best care, but not knowing where to start. In recent years, more and more families in Hong Kong have chosen home care or private nursing services to support their loved ones' self-care and rehabilitation needs after surgery. Whether it's nighttime intensive care or outreach private nursing, the demand is growing. Hygiene First deeply understands that as a Caregiver Training or private nurse, what we bear is not only responsibility, but also a commitment to and care for life.

Decision Stage: Tailor Care Programs to Address Specific Needs
