Potential Risks of Home Nasogastric Tube Feeding

When performing nasogastric tube feeding at home, some families have domestic helpers or family members assist with feeding and medication administration. However, lack of professional knowledge and training can easily lead to overlooking details, resulting in the following potential issues:

Incorrect nasogastric tube positioning, increasing risk of aspiration or choking
Improper operation during feeding or medication administration, potentially causing stomach discomfort, vomiting, or blockage
Insufficient tube hygiene management, easily leading to infection or tube blockage
Neglecting regular checks and care, increasing the chance of complications

Importance of Professional Care

Hygiene First's care team has rich experience and professional skills, providing safe and meticulous nasogastric tube feeding and medication administration services for the elderly and those in need, with key points including:

Strictly check nasogastric tube position to ensure correct placement before each feeding
Prepare and manage feeding formulas and medications according to medical orders, avoiding dosage errors
Flush the tube on schedule to prevent blockage and bacterial growth
Observe patient's physical reactions after feeding and promptly address discomfort or abnormal situations
Provide personalized care advice and assist family members in learning correct care techniques
